## Player route attempt detail

The expansion route runner uses the BP_CarnivalPlayerCharacter in PIE at its normal 450 cm/s walk speed. It registered a test phase and recorded the starting position, but no later player sample or velocity was written. The route is therefore not marked pass or fail as a physical path; the attempt ended before it could determine whether the corrected Prison collision and Lab route are walkable.

The first incorrect runner invocation used Unreal's Python commandlet and did not tick gameplay; that attempt is recorded in Saved/WorldExpansion/walk_routes_engine.log. The later regular-editor invocation still produced only the single starting sample. Its report and live state are Saved/WorldExpansion/Walk_Route_Playtest.json and Walk_Route_Playtest_Live.json. No separate durable stdout log was captured for that later invocation.
